Slide 2 – Objective
Objective of the Trial

The purpose of this trial was to evaluate the capability of the Rubiscape OCR and AI extraction engine to automatically extract engineering datasheet information from Pump and Motor documents.

Goals
Validate OCR extraction accuracy
Compare extracted values against vendor prefilled data
Identify incorrect or missing fields
Measure overall extraction performance
Assess feasibility for production deployment

Slide 8 – Observed Issues
Extraction Issues Identified

Although the extraction accuracy was high, a few issues were observed.

1. OCR Formatting Issues

Example

Vendor

kg/cm2g

Extracted

kg/cm²g

Different formatting although meaning is same.

2. Special Character Differences

Examples

°C
deg C

m3/hr
m³/h

Formatting differences rather than extraction failures.

3. Spacing Issues

Example

Vendor

KSB Limited

Extracted

KSB

or

Deep groove ball bearing06409

Missing spacing between words.

4. Additional OCR Text

Some extracted values contained nearby labels from the PDF.

Example

280S SEE DRIVER DATA SHEET

instead of

280S
5. Incorrect Field Mapping

Few fields were mapped to neighbouring labels.

Example

Slide 9 – Root Cause Analysis
Reasons Behind Incorrect Fields

The identified issues are primarily related to document interpretation rather than OCR failure.

Reasons
Complex engineering document layouts
Multi-line table structures
Adjacent cell reading
OCR formatting variations
Unit normalization differences
Extra contextual text captured
Similar labels positioned closely
Observation

Most incorrect values were not due to missing extraction, but due to field association or formatting differences.
